## PART A — MISSING PICTURES

**1. Tape composition (size-mix / agent fingerprint)** — *flow + behavior*  
CVD and taker imbalance are net signed volume. They erase *who*. A +$40mm CVD of 0.01–0.05 BTC clips is a different animal from eight 50–200 BTC prints. Exhaustion-vs-initiation, stop-run vs informed, retail spray vs warehouse, are in the **mix**, not the sum. Price+OI+funding+CVD treats these as identical. They are not.

**2. Book geometry & resilience (shape, not 5bp stock)** — *structure + flow*  
5bp depth is a *level*. Missing: slope, convexity, bid/ask asymmetry, hole/cliff location, **refill half-life after a sweep**, cancel/replace flicker, microprice vs mark. A thick 5bp that is one-lot deep at 8–20bp, or that evaporates for 800ms after a hit, is not liquidity. Bandwidth-as-stock cannot see **liquidity-as-process**.

**3. Impact / markout / toxicity** — *flow + behavior*  
No λ, no temporary-vs-permanent split, no horizon markout. You cannot tell absorption (aggressive size, price does not keep the tick → passive whale) from thin drift (small size, price walks). CVD up + price up is tautological; **CVD up + markout→0 at +3s/+30s** is information. Without this, “flow” is a rear-view mirror.

**4. Crowd cost-basis & uPnL surface** — *positioning*  
Liq-fuel map = *where stops sit*. Missing: **where inventory was opened** and **how underwater it is**. Same OI at the same mark is inert if the crowd is at-the-money and nuclear if 70% of long OI is −8R with funding bleeding. Forced flow is a function of **distance-to-pain × size × time-held**, not of stop ticks alone. HL makes a proxy reconstructable from fills+positions; you are not using it.

**5. Arrival process (intensity, clustering, clock)** — *behavior*  
Volume and vol-ROC are aggregates. Missing: trade inter-arrival, Hawkes excitation, run-length of same-side takers, **volume-time vs wall-clock**. A 6× volume bar of 400 evenly spaced prints ≠ 6× of three clusters. Cascade ignition is a **clustering** event. Session opens you already watch; you do not residualize against the clock, so TOD seasonality is constantly misread as signal.

**6. Premium–oracle–basis and predicted-vs-printed funding residual** — *structure + positioning*  
You watch signed funding *level*. Missing: mark−oracle path, HL vs coin-volume-weighted CEX index, **predictedFundings vs last printed**, spot-perp on HL (now exists). Funding is a slow tax. Basis/premium is the *instantaneous* dislocation the tax is trying to close. When they disagree, funding is lying about the marginal unit.

**7. Realized-vol geometry (RV, jump share, efficiency)** — *structure*  
No RV, no bipower/jump decomposition, no vol-of-vol, no Kaufman efficiency / range-per-dollar. A 0.4% walk on 6× volume is distribution; a 0.4% walk with 70% jump-share is a discrete event (liq, oracle, venue outage). Your velocity/accel live in a diffusion mental model. Perps die in jumps.

**8. Microstructure factor residual** — *cross-asset, but not your 24h β*  
24h lead-lag is too slow and too correlated-to-be-useful. Missing: **1s–5m residual of the name vs BTC-perp / vs HL-basket**, and which venue is the information share. If the name is a pure β-print, your OI/CVD read is borrowed from BTC and will unwind when the factor does. If residual is the thing moving, the opposite.

**9. LP inventory–quote feedback (HLP + apparent MM)** — *positioning + behavior*  
HLP equity+inventory is a stock. Missing: **how quotes respond to HLP PnL path** — spread, skew, depth refill, which side they lean, whether HLP is mean-reverting inventory or running a hole. When the house MM is the book, their inventory cycle *is* the next 30–90m of drift and of cascade convexity. You own the data; you watch the level.

**10. Joint residual / path of (price, OI, funding, CVD)** — *structure*  
You read four margins. The information is in the **copula and the sequence**. “OI fell on 6× volume” is one path-object you already used. You do not have a systematic library of path-objects (OI↑ price↓ funding→0 vs OI↑ price↓ funding↑, etc.) nor the residual after regressing each on the others. Same snapshot, opposite futures, different paths.

**11. OI concentration, cohort, cross-margin graph** — *positioning* — *semi-costly* (public chain/API, needs indexer + address clustering).  
Top-N share of OI, isolated vs cross, overlapping collateral across BTC/ETH/SOL/HYPE, new-wallet vs veteran, builder-code segmented flow. A 41k OI that is 40 addresses is a detonation device; the same OI in 4,000 is a fog. Cross-margin makes *your BTC liq map incomplete*: the trigger can be HYPE. Justify cost: one-time indexer, then it is the highest-Sharpe positioning layer HL uniquely offers.

**12. Cross-venue basis / OI / funding / depth** — *structure* — *cheap-external* (Binance/Bybit public).  
HL-only tape can be the satellite. If HL premium, funding, and OI delta diverge from the 80% venue, HL signals describe HL positioning, not the contract. You will liquidate (or be run over) on an island.

**Costly, do not prioritize:** Deribit GEX/IV-skew, ETF creations, CEX on-chain exchange flows, social attention, full TradFi order flow. GEX matters on event days; it does not beat (1)–(10) on a random Tuesday on HL.

## PART B — HOW TO LOOK AT EACH (2–3 transforms, exact emit)

**1. Tape composition**  
- *Size-bucket signed volume* (e.g. <1σ, 1–3σ, >3σ of 1h print-size). Signal: **large-bucket Δ diverges from small-bucket Δ** → informed/warehouse vs spray.  
- *Gini / Herfindahl of notional in the last N prints* (and its 15m Δ). Signal: **HHI↑ while CVD flat** → stealth one-sided warehouse, CVD blind.  
- *Print-to-tick ratio* (trades per price change) by bucket. Signal: **large prints, low ticks** = absorption; **small prints, high ticks** = thin walk. Opposite futures.

**2. Book geometry & resilience**  
- *Multi-band depth vector* (1/2/5/10/25bp) + **log-slope and convexity** each side, and bid−ask slope gap. Signal: **convexity flip** (belly pulled, wings left) = optional-hedge / crash book, not “depth down.”  
- *Sweep-refill half-life*: time and % of depth restored at 5bp and 15bp after each >X lot hit. Signal: **τ↑ or restore%<50%** = MM pull; next unit of CVD gets a discrete jump in impact.  
- *Microprice − mark − oracle* triad, 1s. Signal: **microprice leads mark, oracle lags** = HL-native pressure; **oracle leads microprice** = you are following CEX, fade HL-only book.

**3. Impact / toxicity**  
- *Kyle λ* rolling: Δmid / signed-notional, 5s and 60s windows; **λ_5s / λ_60s**. Signal: ratio→1 = permanent (informed); ratio→0 = temporary (inventory, will revert).  
- *Horizon markout* of each taker print at +1s/+5s/+30s/+5m, signed, size-weighted. Signal: **markout curve inverts** (winners at +1s become losers at +5m) = flow just flipped from toxic to exhausted.  
- *VPIN-style bucket toxicity* on volume-clock (equal-notional bars). Signal: **VPIN > local 90th and λ_60s rising** = MMs about to skew/pull *before* 5bp depth prints the pull.

**4. Cost-basis / uPnL surface**  
- *Volume-weighted entry of open OI* (reconstruct from trade tape + OI Δ attribution; crude: OI-up bars’ VWAP as “new inventory”). Signal: **mark − VWAP_open as σ-distance**, split by inferred side.  
- *Underwater mass*: fraction of inferred OI with uPnL < −k·ATR, and **its 15m Δ**. Signal: **mass↑ while price velocity→0** = coil; next velocity unit hits a convex forced-flow function, not a linear one.  
- *Pain gradient*: d(uPnL_mass)/d(mark) numerically from the surface. Signal: **gradient steepens on one side only** = that side’s next stop-run is self-financing; the other is not. Different from “stops sit at X.”

**5. Arrival / clock**  
- *Hawkes kernel*: excitation α, decay β on same-side taker events. Signal: **α/β ↑ through 1** = self-exciting; cascade *conditional probability* just flipped, price has not.  
- *Run length*: consecutive same-side taker prints / notional. Signal: **run-length CDF right-tail fattening** with CVD slope unchanged = hidden persistence your ROC misses.  
- *Volume-time resample* of everything you already compute (CVD, OI Δ, funding). Signal: **wall-clock signal dies in volume-time** = session artifact; **survives and steepens** = real. Also emit **clock-residual volume** (actual / TOD-seasonal). Surprise volume, not volume.

**6. Premium / predicted funding**  
- *d(premium)/dt vs printed funding vs predictedFundings*. Signal: **premium expanding while predicted funding still low** = tax has not repriced; squeeze/unwind *fuel is being under-charged*. Opposite: premium collapsing onto a still-high printed funding = tax is now punitive on a dead move.  
- *HL mark − CEX-index residual, z-scored on 1h*. Signal: **|z| > 2 with HL depth thin** = island; HL liq map will fire without CEX confirmation, or will not fire when CEX does.  
- *Carry inconsistency*: funding − (basis / horizon) − r_f. Signal: **residual ≠ 0 and widening** = arb capital absent (constraint); dislocation can trend. Residual mean-reverting = arb on, fade the basis.

**7. Vol geometry**  
- *RV vs bipower variation* on 1s→5m; **jump share J = 1 − BV/RV**. Signal: **J↑ through ~0.4** = discard diffusion reads (CVD slope, λ_60s); switch to discrete-event playbook (liq, oracle, venue).  
- *vol-of-vol*: |ΔRV| / RV, and RV term structure (5m vs 1h vs 4h). Signal: **short RV >> long RV** = burst, mean-revert vol; **inverted and persistent** = regime change, your lookbacks are wrong.  
- *Efficiency ratio* (net move / path length) on volume-time. Signal: **ER↓ with volume↑** = churn, OI likely transferring not directional; **ER↑ with volume↑** = informed one-way. This is the missing quality flag on your “6× volume.”

**8. Factor residual**  
- *Rolling 30s–5m residual* r_name − β̂ r_BTC (β on volume-time). Signal: **residual velocity dominates** = idiosyncratic positioning (your OI/funding local); **residual ~0, factor velocity dominates** = ignore name-local OI.  
- *Information share / lead-lag in 100–500ms* HL-BTC vs HL-name vs (if external) CEX-BTC. Signal: **name leads BTC** = rotation/idio; **CEX leads HL** = you are late.  
- *β instability*: |dβ/dt|. Signal: **β break** = correlation regime change; cross-asset lens you already have just went stale, and so did every residual.

**9. LP / HLP feedback**  
- *HLP inventory Δ vs subsequent 30–90m signed mid* (conditional response function). Signal: **response flips sign** (they stop fading their inventory) = house no longer mean-reverts; drift persists.  
- *Spread + skew as function of HLP uPnL path* (not level). Signal: **spread↑ and skew with HLP underwater** = they are hedging *themselves*; book will not catch a knife on that side.  
- *Refill τ (from #2) regressed on HLP inventory*. Signal: **τ sensitive to HLP** = they *are* the 5bp; their equity line is a leading indicator of bandwidth collapse, 5bp depth is lagging.

**10. Joint residual / path**  
- *Vector residual*: regress each of {Δp, ΔOI, funding, ΔCVD} on the other three; trade the **innovation**. Signal: **OI innovation <0 with volume innovation >0 and Δp innovation ~0** = transfer/exhaustion (you already saw one instance; this *systematizes* it).  
- *Path lexicon* (encode last 30–120m as a symbol string of joint-bin moves). Signal: **current string’s historical successor distribution** — not a price mean, a *distribution over next path-objects* (cascade-prone, grind, two-way, island).  
- *Copula tail*: P(ΔOI extreme | Δp extreme) vs independent. Signal: **upper-tail dep ↑** = leverage on, next move self-feeds; **tail dep ↓ while OI high** = leverage already used, same OI is dead.

**11. Concentration / contagion** *(semi-costly)*  
- *Top-N OI share + isolated/cross split*, 5m. Signal: **top-N share↑** = single-name liquidation becomes a *market* event.  
- *Collateral overlap graph* (addresses with BTC + HYPE + SOL). Signal: **edge-weight↑** = your BTC liq map is missing the true trigger.  
- *Cohort flow*: new-wallet taker notional vs veteran. Signal: **new-wallet share↑ into a move** = late, high-pain inventory being created *now* (feeds #4 with a 30–90m lag).

**12. Cross-venue** *(cheap-external)*  
- *z(HL − BIN mid), z(HL funding − BIN funding), z(HL OI Δ − BIN OI Δ)*. Signal: **all three same sign** = global; **HL-only** = island, local signals decay.  
- *Cross-venue depth-share*. Signal: **HL depth-share↓ while HL volume-share↑** = toxic venue, λ about to jump.  
- *First-to-move on jumps* (who prints the 1s impulse). Signal: persistent CEX-first = do not let HL CVD initiate.

## PART C — CONFLUENCE / COMBINATION / CONTRADICTION

Doctrine: a single series forecasts nothing. The forward object is a **regime of the *next unit of flow***: impact it will get, whether it self-excites, whether it is island or global, whether pain is convex. Direction is not the output; **the mapping from next-dollar to next-state** is.

### Confluent pairs (reinforce — raise conviction in a *state*)
- **Hawkes α/β > 1 AND refill τ↑ AND λ_5s/λ_60s → 1** → self-exciting *informed* flow into a pulling book. Next unit of CVD is a jump, not a walk. Existing velocity/accel will *under-read* the next 60–180s.  
- **Underwater mass↑ (one side) AND pain-gradient steepening AND tail-dependence(ΔOI, Δp)↑** → forced-flow function is armed. Liq-fuel map is no longer a static heatmap; it is a live trigger.  
- **ER↑ on volume-time AND large-bucket signed Δ agrees with small-bucket AND residual-vs-BTC dominates** → idio, informed, one-way quality. Participation (your OI/volume) is *real*.  
- **HLP response-function sign-flip AND spread/skew tracks HLP uPnL AND 5bp convexity flip** → house is no longer a mean-reverting shock-absorber. Your bandwidth lens will print “OK” until it prints “gone.”  
- **HL−CEX z-scores aligned AND CEX-first on impulses AND predicted funding repricing with premium** → global tax/dislocation. Local HL OI reads travel.

- **Absorption detector**: large-bucket CVD *extreme* + print-to-tick *low* + markout_{+30s}→0 + refill
